The new private tables retain comparison bindings to those decisions; they do not extract claims, group events, publish source articles, infer Story IDs or replace those engines.

`comparison_reviewed_members` binds one existing event membership to the exact latest article admission ID, retained capture and hash. Source URLs, outlets and publication/fetch/capture clocks come from that admission's immutable snapshot. Navigation records freeze the existing admitted arc and event-node records. Pending captures do not supersede an admission. A newer admission decision prevents fallback to an older member binding, including when the newer decision is unavailable. A Source Report may be an explicitly reviewed metadata member and has unknown claim coverage; it cannot be a proposition surface, correction support or evidence-link support.

`comparison_reviewed_surfaces` references the shared `(public_version_id, article_claim_id)` evidence key. Every selected annotation inherits the bound proposition capture and span. A changed native annotation closes its bound surface rather than emitting new current bytes. No public caller or ingestion-service assertion can qualify a binding.

Existing opaque article/claim/event keys, missing-extraction versus omission behavior, correction presentation, E1/E2/E4 semantics and pagination remain.
