transaction modes require exactly one case-insensitive Preference-Applied
header with one exact `tx=commit` token, HTTP201 and an empty non-NULL body.
Rollback mode, absent/duplicate/ambiguous commit tokens and NULL body refuse.
The sender may normalize a zero-length extension body at that ABI boundary,
but neither that normalization nor an applied-commit header proves storage.

absent. Resolving this requires a separately reviewed event/version-specific
receiver and independent post-commit observation contract before live use.
