# Owner-operated physical-device acceptance

No item below is pre-filled as passed. Use one record per actual hardware/browser,
orientation, input, network/account and exact candidate/source/camera baseline.
Record failures and unknowns before retrying; later evidence supplements history.

1. Pin the exact candidate commit/tree, build/config and harness versions. Record
   actual iPad/iPhone/Desktop model, OS/browser version, viewport/DPR, orientation,
   touch/pointer/keyboard method, account alias/readiness, network/cache conditions,
   operator and UTC time. Retain screenshots/video/log hashes. Do not record tokens.
2. Separate FUNCTIONAL, VISUAL and PERFORMANCE verdicts. A functional pass does
   not imply final appearance or hardware performance. Browser emulation stays
   preliminary and cannot fill physical FPS or thermal results.
3. On primary iPad, open News first. Read Home/Breaking, Feed, a reviewed Story and
   retained historical source version. Check separate publication/fetch/capture/
   event clocks, deterministic reason and visibly attributed SOURCE REPORT state.
   Open evidence/provenance; verify exact capture/hash/version and no raw/private
   or latest substitute. Record unknown coverage honestly.
4. Follow the displayed Story, acknowledge that displayed version, and reopen it
   from in-app Following. Confirm durable preference after foreground refresh.
   Exercise two authorized existing accounts, sign-out, expiry/readiness and a
   deliberately delayed A request while B is active. A's personal data must clear
   immediately. External email/push is post-launch and is not required here.
5. From each required surface open Explore/Search; type, filter, scroll and dismiss.
   Compare canonical subject, selected reviewed version, exact recorded time/range
   and focus before/after. They must stay unchanged until one explicit result
   selection. Record compatible sub-selection propagation; unsupported selection
   must disclose the fallback to its parent.
   If optional user-declared location is available, choose only city/region/country,
   clear it back to neutral Feed and compare global versus personal relevance.
   Clearing it also clears the cached local lens; a public URL must not expose a
   home location. Discovery filters must not drop counterevidence from the subject.
   No IP inference, device geolocation or browser location permission is permitted.
   Where absent, record unavailable; this checklist creates no new UI or engine.
6. Take that investigation through actual Graph, Map and Split, World View, Story
   return, browser Back/Forward and a shareable deep link. Compare recorded context
   fields; check related endpoints and source versions. Context must not silently
   drift because panels open, a camera moves or a result preview changes.
   Review Timeline/Arc stage versus event identity, source provenance, separate
   confidence dimensions, corrections and retained history. Private research,
   hypotheses, preferences and workspace state never become canonical evidence.
   Keep valid/event time distinct from known/as-of time and verify later corrections
   do not leak into an earlier point-in-time view. Unavailable owner state stays unknown.
7. In World View capture each supported density state: far icon/cluster, approach
   ribbon, precision-honest near plaque, selected tethered screen-space card,
   Context/Sources/Evidence, Inspector, close and reopen. Canonical coordinates,
   version, time, precision class and legal camera floor remain immutable.
   The physical 1,200m near band remains unsupported by the minimum 8,660.254m
   facility floor. Use supported scope density at its existing legal floor; never
   lower it or relabel a scope plaque as precise evidence. Record occlusion,
   attribution and the canonical tether, including offscreen/occluded anchors.
8. Repeat the iPad journey in both physical orientations while a card/Inspector,
   Options, Explore chooser and long source disclosure are open. Preserve context
   and camera; check real touch gestures, map space, card body scroll, close,
   Inspector and native credits. Record screen bounds and input evidence.
9. On iPhone repeat portrait, landscape and narrow/coarse-touch constrained states.
   Check actual tap, drag, pinch, scroll ownership, focus, long titles/disclosures,
   options, map/credits and selected-card controls. A 44px source requirement stays
   unchanged; a browser touchscreen success is not actual multitouch acceptance.
10. On Desktop inspect useful information density and full investigation return.
    Traverse navigation, discovery, mode tabs, selected-card modules and Inspector
    with ordinary Tab/Shift+Tab/Enter/Space/Escape. Verify visible focus, order,
    focus return, screen reader names/state and scrolling without a pointer.
11. Background the real app/browser, resume, then Map→Graph→Map and re-enter the
    same subject. Record camera/context and source status before/after; stale
    photographic ACTIVE disclosure must clear until current attachment is proven.
    Verify obsolete callbacks do not repaint and old renderer ownership releases.
12. Disable the actual network after current data and during a pending read.
    Exercise source/weather/publication failures and an explicit retry. Require
    truthful unavailable/unknown states, default-deny evidence, no current-time or
    latest-version fill, no repeated hidden provider request and no fabricated
    account result. Use authorized test conditions; never alter publication live.
13. Exercise a supported WebGL-unavailable/loss fixture and remount. Record actual
    renderer/fallback, context, legal floor, disclosure and errors. Do not describe
    a scripted startup refusal as a physical GPU failure. Keep real loss evidence
    separate from browser emulation.
14. VISUAL: after real imagery, terrain and geometry have their own lawful admission,
    pin each exact source version/provider/provenance and capture permissions.
    Hold subject/version/recorded-time/camera/mode/content/panel/viewport constant
    across comparisons. Owner reviews actual iPad/iPhone/Desktop pixels and labels.
    Synthetic procedural geometry is behavioral evidence only. Do not infer event
    time, accuracy, coverage or currentness from photographic appearance.
15. PERFORMANCE: first recover the original executable cold-portrait boundary or
    obtain an explicit replacement measurement contract. Retain historical RED:
    portrait cold 14→26, unchanged delta-under-12 budget; unit/window/readiness are
    unrecovered. Record original/replacement authority rather than inventing units.
    For each measured quantity retain instrument/method/window/raw evidence/cache/
    network/subject/version/source/camera and thermal/ambient/charging/low-power
    conditions. Unknown FPS, GPU cost, memory and thermals stay unknown. Record
    sustained interaction separately from startup and settled idle. Do not loosen
    the threshold or claim a pass from CPU layout or screenshot timing.
16. Finish each record with exact artifacts, limitations, blockers, operator and
    owner verdict. Validate it against the schema and semantic gates. Physical
    device operation, authentic source admission, installed Auth/backend, hosting,
    merge/deployment and release approval remain separately authorized work.
