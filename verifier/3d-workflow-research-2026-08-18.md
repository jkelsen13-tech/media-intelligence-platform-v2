Magnific’s official plan documentation was opened but did not finish rendering before the paired browser-review save point; its connected-account gate remains the controlling fact for this task.

No external 3D service, subscription, or paid credit has been activated.

## Magnific pricing reconciliation

Magnific’s public pricing page currently presents paid Premium, Premium+, and Pro tiers; the entry premium price shown is **$14.50/month billed annually** (excluding VAT/local taxes). The page includes Magnific MCP and API access among paid-plan benefits. This accords with the actual connected-account result in this task: the MCP rejected an account-balance request and said a premium account is required. In other words, the public service may publish limited free exploration in documentation, but the currently connected automation path is not available on the present account without upgrading. 

**Decision:** the completed Blender scene is the appropriate zero-cost, fully editable source of truth. Magnific would only be worth considering later for creative texture exploration or upscale variants after an owner elects a paid plan; it is neither necessary nor preferable for a controlled geometric logo.
