## Owner ordering/recovery proposal — not accepted
- Completion obtains final acceptance locks first: allow that transaction to commit; revocation waits. If completion rolls back, it has no accepted output or acknowledgement.
- Revocation commits before final acceptance: reject completion and roll back its output, acknowledgement and receipt together.
- Outstanding lease: conveys no authority after principal/session revocation. A later completion is denied.
- Stranded processing: preserve lease/input/output/history; do not silently reset or transfer. Reconcile under an explicitly approved recovery capability after expiry and fresh authorization/evaluation.
- Retry: identical same-owner request may replay only while currently authorized. An unknown completion outcome requires reconciliation/identical retry, not a new output or forced failure. Renewed sessions do not change runtime/principal ownership.
- Existing isolated retry timings/counts are qualification policy only. Owner must choose production retry classification, backoff/limits, quarantine/escalation and recovery operator.
- Force-cancellation: proposed default prohibited. Any emergency exception needs separately authorized operator, reason, transaction handling and append-only audit; never erase accepted history.
- Restoration after suspension/withdrawal: fresh identity/evaluation and full current evidence/privacy/rights closure; explicit new selection/approval. No automatic resurrection of prior publication.
Owner must approve or replace this policy; behaving as designed in isolation is not approval.
